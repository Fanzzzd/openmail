## Why does Google show an unverified app warning?

Gmail access needs scopes that Google classifies as restricted (`gmail.modify` and `gmail.send`). Google verifies apps that request restricted scopes through a review that includes a third-party security assessment. OpenMail has not completed that review yet, so Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app" on the sign-in page.

To continue, click **Advanced**, then **Go to OpenMail (unsafe)**. The warning is about the review status. It does not change what the app can do: OpenMail still gets only the permissions listed on the consent screen, and your mail still goes only to your Mac. Google limits unverified apps to 100 users.

If you prefer not to rely on OpenMail's Google project, [register your own OAuth client](/docs/own-oauth-client).

## Why do I need to sign in again?

OpenMail pauses an account and asks you to sign in again only when the provider gives a definite answer that the saved sign-in no longer works. Network errors, timeouts and server errors are retried instead.

| Message in the app                                                   | Cause                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Google currently limits OpenMail's sign-in to 7 days                 | OpenMail's Google project is in Google's testing mode, where Google expires refresh tokens after 7 days. This ends when the project is published. |
| Sign-in was revoked or has expired                                   | You removed OpenMail's access in your Google or Microsoft account, changed your password, or the token expired.                                   |
| Needs to grant mail permissions again                                | A permission OpenMail needs was not granted.                                                                                                      |
| The OAuth client that signed in this account is no longer configured | You removed or replaced the OAuth client that issued this account's token.                                                                        |
| Saved sign-in is missing from the keychain                           | The Keychain entry for the account was deleted.                                                                                                   |
| The mail server rejected the password                                | The IMAP or SMTP server rejected the app-specific password.                                                                                       |

Microsoft expires refresh tokens for personal accounts after 90 days without use. While the app is open, OpenMail refreshes each account's token about every 6 days, even for paused accounts, so this limit is not reached.

## Where is my mail stored?

In `~/Library/Application Support/openmail` on your Mac. Sign-in tokens and passwords are in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is stored on an OpenMail server, because there is none.

## Does OpenMail collect analytics?

No. OpenMail has no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for every server the app can contact.

## Can I use a work or school Microsoft 365 account?

No. Those accounts need an administrator to approve the app for the organization. OpenMail supports personal Microsoft accounts only.

## How do I remove OpenMail's access to my account?

Remove the account in **Settings → Accounts**, which deletes its local mail and Keychain entry. For Gmail, OpenMail also asks Google to revoke its access; if that fails, remove OpenMail at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Microsoft has no such revocation for OpenMail, so remove it yourself at [account.live.com/consent/Manage](https://account.live.com/consent/Manage). See [Removing an account](/docs/accounts#removing-an-account).
